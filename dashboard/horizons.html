What is predictable, from 1 month to 10 years

Descriptive horizon study; both holdouts were spent before it was designed, so no number here is out-of-sample evidence and nothing was selected on it. The published 12-month forecast and the frozen models are unchanged.

Returns

Nothing reliable at any horizon

Up to 12 months the pooled R squared against the historical mean is -4.0% to +1.3% in 1990 to 2014 and -4.0% to +3.2% after 2015. It reaches +7.3% at 3 to 5 years before 2015, on about five independent windows that the later years cannot confirm.

Volatility

Predictable out to about a year

The blended forecast removed 48% to 53% of the naive loss at 1 to 12 months in 1990 to 2014. After 2015 the average is -13% to 9% because of the March 2020 crash, though it was closer in 73% to 84% of months.

Correlations

Modestly predictable, and it held up

The blended forecast removed 14% to 23% of the naive squared error in 1990 to 2014 and 22% to 35% after 2015.

Combined

No reliable gain for the whole model

Sizing positions with both forecasts changed the certainty-equivalent return by -0.3% to +0.5% a year in 1990 to 2014 and -0.2% to +0.2% after 2015, depending on the horizon.

Is it worth money?

Single-asset sleeves against cash, each forecast held for its own horizon, after trading costs. Gain in annualized certainty-equivalent return over the baseline (historical mean with long-run volatility), averaged over the 12 sleeves. Rows are return forecast + volatility forecast.

Long-only minimum-variance portfolio built from the forecasts: realized volatility relative to the same portfolio built from naive forecasts. Below 1 means the forecasts lowered risk.

How to read this

  • Forecasts are made at every month end and scored once their window has closed. 1990 to 2014 are the years the package's models were developed on; 2015 on are years already used once as a holdout. Neither column is a test.
  • Return forecasters: sig_combo averages nine single-signal regressions; hz_ridge, hz_gbm and hz_ens are the frozen learners re-aimed at each horizon under new names. The bar is the expanding historical mean.
  • Risk forecasters: trail_N is the last N months; half averages a trailing window with the long-run level; har is a log regression; published is the package's selected model at its own 12-month horizon. The bar is the long-run level.
  • Nothing is adjusted for the number of cells. With this many, isolated good cells are expected by chance.
  • Ten-year cells are mostly blank: 25 years hold at most two and a half non-overlapping windows.
  • Full method and tables: reports/horizon_study.md, results/horizons/.
