    How the point is picked

    • Each goal states the largest fall from a previous high it can sit through.
    • The stock share is the largest whose worst fall in the replay stayed within that, with the rest in the sleeve.
    • At 100% stocks nothing is left to backfill, so the sleeve goes on top, 50% of capital: on a margin loan at T-bills + 1.38% in a taxable account, inside stacked funds in a retirement account.
    • Money needed within 3 years is outside the model and gets T-bills.

    What this does not show

    • The loss limits are judgements, and the stock shares follow from them. The table above shows what any other limit would give.
    • So is the 50% of sleeve on top of 100% stocks. The loss limit alone would allow any amount the line reaches.
    • Every year in the replay had been seen before the rule was written, the three diversifiers were chosen on the same years, and managed futures before 2007-03 are a paper factor. A worst fall in the past does not bound the next one.
    • Month-end values miss falls inside a month, and a margin loan can be called before a month ends.
    • Taxes, trading costs and fund fees are not charged, so tax-deferred and Roth accounts get the same numbers.
    • This is arithmetic on the package's studies, not advice.
