How much in stocks: the fall you can sit through decides

Stocks cause the big falls, so the fall your goal can sit through sets how much goes in stocks. Intermediate Treasuries, gold and managed futures held up when stocks fell, so they take the rest of the capital. Nothing is borrowed, and expected returns decide nothing. Forecast origin 2026-10-31.

One way to hold it

    Funds and fees are a hand-recorded lookup from 2026-11-16, using the cheapest recorded funds that fit. Confirm on the issuer's page. No stock fund's fee is recorded.

    How the mix is set

    1. Stocks: the largest share, in steps of 5%, whose worst fall in the replay stayed within the goal's drawdown budget. The fall is measured on the whole portfolio, diversifiers included.
    2. Diversifiers: the rest of the capital, always 44% Treasuries, 19% gold, 37% managed futures. Nothing is borrowed.
    3. Money needed within 3 years has no budget and stays in T-bills.

    What this does not show

    • The drawdown budgets are judgements, and the stock shares follow from them. The table above shows what any other budget would give.
    • The diversifiers fall too. With no stocks at all, the diversifiers alone fell -9% in the replay.
    • Every year in the replay had been seen before the rule was written, the diversifiers were chosen on the same years, and managed futures before 2007-03 are a paper factor. A worst fall in the past does not bound the next one.
    • Month-end values miss falls inside a month. Taxes, trading costs and fund fees are not charged.
    • This is arithmetic on the package's studies, not advice.
