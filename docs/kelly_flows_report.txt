The lifecycle bound: future deposits are a bond you already own
====================================================================================================
   If the Kelly fraction is the right share of TOTAL economic wealth, and total
   wealth is the account plus the present value of what you will still pay in,
   then the leverage to run on the account alone is L x (1 + PV/account).
                          deposits PV of deposits implied leverage
horizon                                                           
10y      20%/yr of opening balance          1.73x            4.31x
20y      20%/yr of opening balance          3.10x            6.48x
30y      20%/yr of opening balance          4.20x            8.21x
20y      40%/yr of opening balance          6.21x           11.39x

   Reg-T caps you at 2x. For a young saver the binding constraint is the broker,
   not Kelly -- and the assumption underneath is that a salary is a Treasury bond,
   which it is not. Treat this as an upper bound, never a target.
