    When was any edge earned or lost?

    Cumulative squared error of the historical mean minus that of the model (the Goyal-Welch plot). The line rises in months the model was closer and falls when it was further away; a line ending above zero means the model won over the whole sample.

    Evidence · predictive distribution

    Do the distribution forecasts beat a volatility baseline?

    The fair bar for a distribution forecast is not the historical mean, whose width never changes, but a forecast that simply tracks recent volatility (the EWMA volatility baseline). Beating the historical mean on CRPS mostly means tracking volatility; beating the volatility baseline means adding something to it.
